## Overview

Modern lifestyle and vibrant vibe marketplace homepage assembled from refined modern header, vibrant trendsetter hero carousel, trending highlights grid, lifestyle promotional banner, curated products feed, and footer.

### Component Chain
`home-25: header-06 -> carousel-30 -> productlist-02 -> promotion-11 -> productlist-01 -> footer-02`

### Included Components
| Component | Custom Element | Role & Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Header-06` | `<header-06>` | Modern lifestyle marketplace sticky navigation header with search and shortcuts |
| `Carousel-30` | `<carousel-30>` | Vibrant trendsetter hero banner carousel with bold aesthetic imagery |
| `ProductList-02` | `<productlist-02>` | Trending lifestyle highlights and bestseller products grid |
| `Promotion-11` | `<promotion-11>` | Vibrant lifestyle market celebration promotional marketing banner |
| `ProductList-01` | `<productlist-01>` | Multi-category curated lifestyle products grid with quick add-to-cart |
| `Footer-02` | `<footer-02>` | Lifestyle market community, service guarantees, and customer care footer |

### Dimensions & Responsiveness
- Aligned to global content width (`w-full max-w-content mx-auto px-4 lg:px-6`).
- Mobile: Fully responsive stacked layout with native touch carousel gestures.
