## Overview

Promotion-driven retail e-commerce homepage designed for high conversion, assembled from promo-focused navigation header, hero carousel, bestselling grid, promotional banner, curated product feed, and footer.

### Component Chain
`home-04: header-04 -> carousel-03 -> productlist-02 -> promotion-02 -> productlist-01 -> footer-02`

### Included Components
| Component | Custom Element | Role & Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Header-04` | `<header-04>` | Promo-focused sticky navigation bar with countdown announcement and action controls |
| `Carousel-03` | `<carousel-03>` | Hero responsive banner carousel highlighting major marketing campaigns |
| `ProductList-02` | `<productlist-02>` | Featured bestseller products grid showcase with quick add-to-cart |
| `Promotion-02` | `<promotion-02>` | Promotional discount banner reinforcing promotional campaign incentives |
| `ProductList-01` | `<productlist-01>` | Multi-category product showcase grid for comprehensive product discovery |
| `Footer-02` | `<footer-02>` | Global site footer with customer trust badges and sitemap navigation |

### Dimensions & Responsiveness
- Aligned to global content width (`w-full max-w-content mx-auto px-4 lg:px-6`).
- Mobile: Fully responsive stacked layout with native touch carousel gestures.
