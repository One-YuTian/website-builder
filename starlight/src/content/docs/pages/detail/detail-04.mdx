## Overview

Product Detail Page (PDP), integrating multi-view gallery preview, purchase decision region (title, price comparison, SKU swatches, quantity stepper, Add to Cart/Buy Now), and bottom accordion tabs.

### Layout & Detailed Region Breakdown
- `gallery`: **Left multi-view gallery region**, containing large main product image viewport (supports hover zoom or image carousel) and 4~5 thumbnail skeletons below or on the left.
- `summary`: **Right purchase decision core region**, containing product title, brand badge, 5-star rating, bold sale price and strikethrough original price, color swatches, size selector, quantity stepper (- 1 +), Add to Cart and Buy Now buttons, and trust badges.
- `accordion-tabs`: **Bottom detailed information accordion region**, containing product Description, Specifications table, Shipping & Returns policy, and Customer Reviews.

### Dimensions & Responsiveness
- Follows `content-container` with PC max content width 1200px centered.
- Desktop (1024px+): 1:1 two-column layout (gallery ~550px, summary ~550px).
- Mobile (under 1024px): Adapts to single vertical column with swipeable gallery.

### Attributes & Props
| Attribute | Description | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `productId` | Product ID displayed | `string` | `'product-01'` |

### Events
- `product-navigate`: Dispatched when clicking recommended product.
- `gallery-change`: Dispatched when switching thumbnail view.
