## Overview

Promotional banner and coupon campaign showcase block, covering side-by-side grid, cinematic wide banner, coupon vouchers, and special discount highlights to drive marketing conversion.

### Layout & Regions
- `banner-container`: Main banner visual container with background image skeleton and text overlay.
- `coupon-group`: Perforated coupon cards with discount amount and Collect buttons.
- `cta-button`: High-contrast promotional action button.

### Dimensions & Responsiveness
- Follows global design system, outer container uses `content-container` with PC max content width 1200px centered.
- Desktop (1024px+): Multi-column side-by-side banners or wide cinematic banner.
- Mobile (under 1024px): Stacks into single-column vertical flow with full width.

### Attributes & Props
| Attribute | Description | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `layout` | Banner layout mode | `'split' \| 'full'` | `'split'` |

### Events
- `promotion-navigate`: Dispatched when clicking promotional banner or coupon voucher.
