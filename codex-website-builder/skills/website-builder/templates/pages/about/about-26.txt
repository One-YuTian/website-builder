## Overview

About Us brand introduction and story page, featuring brand Hero banner visual skeleton, core mission & vision statements, service feature highlights grid, and company timeline / team credentials to build customer brand trust.

### Layout & Regions
- `hero`: Top full-width banner visual skeleton with centered text overlay.
- `intro`: Centered brand introduction and mission statement text skeleton area.
- `benefits-grid`: Service guarantee cards (2 cols on tablet, 4 cols on desktop).
- `milestones`: Brand history timeline or team credentials showcase grid.

### Dimensions & Responsiveness
- Follows global design system, outer container uses `content-container` with PC max content width 1200px centered.
- Mobile (under 1024px): Stacks into single-column vertical flow.

### Attributes & Props
| Attribute | Description | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | Theme styling mode | `'light' \| 'dark'` | `'light'` |

### Events
- `about-navigate`: Dispatched when clicking action buttons or links within the About section.
