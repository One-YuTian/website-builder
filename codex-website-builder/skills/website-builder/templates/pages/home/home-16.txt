## Overview

Contemporary furniture and whole-home interior homepage assembled from standard navigation header, furniture category grid, living and bedroom featured furniture showcase, home accessories grid, delivery and assembly service bar, and footer.

### Component Chain
`home-16: header-01 -> categories-04 -> productlist-02 -> productlist-01 -> services-03 -> footer-01`

### Included Components
| Component | Custom Element | Role & Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Header-01` | `<header-01>` | Standard sticky navigation header with search bar, categories, and cart |
| `Categories-04` | `<categories-04>` | Furniture and room space category grid (living room, bedroom, dining, study) |
| `ProductList-02` | `<productlist-02>` | Featured living room and bedroom large furniture showcase grid |
| `ProductList-01` | `<productlist-01>` | Home decor accessories and textile goods showcase grid |
| `Services-03` | `<services-03>` | Heavy furniture free delivery, in-home assembly, and multi-year warranty service bar |
| `Footer-01` | `<footer-01>` | Global furniture e-commerce footer with consultation links and newsletter signup |

### Dimensions & Responsiveness
- Aligned to global content width (`w-full max-w-content mx-auto px-4 lg:px-6`).
- Mobile: Fully responsive stacked layout with native touch carousel gestures.
