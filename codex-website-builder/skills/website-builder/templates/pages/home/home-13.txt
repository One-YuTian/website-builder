## Overview

Streetwear and urban hype fashion homepage assembled from modern marketplace navigation header, urban street style hero carousel, streetwear style category cards, limited collaboration promotional banner, hype fashion products grid, and footer.

### Component Chain
`home-13: header-05 -> carousel-16 -> categories-03 -> promotion-01 -> productlist-01 -> footer-02`

### Included Components
| Component | Custom Element | Role & Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Header-05` | `<header-05>` | Modern marketplace navigation header tailored for urban trendsetters |
| `Carousel-16` | `<carousel-16>` | Urban street style hero carousel showcasing avant-garde visual aesthetic |
| `Categories-03` | `<categories-03>` | Streetwear style category cards for targeted apparel and sneaker exploration |
| `Promotion-01` | `<promotion-01>` | Limited collaboration capsule collection promotional banner |
| `ProductList-01` | `<productlist-01>` | Hype street fashion products grid showcase with quick purchase actions |
| `Footer-02` | `<footer-02>` | Urban fashion community and customer trust footer |

### Dimensions & Responsiveness
- Aligned to global content width (`w-full max-w-content mx-auto px-4 lg:px-6`).
- Mobile: Fully responsive stacked layout with native touch carousel gestures.
